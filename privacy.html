Authorization data is used solely to authenticate and upload videos at the direction of the channel operator. The app does not request Google account passwords or sell personal data.

OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be stored privately in the operator's application environment, not on this public website or in public source repositories. Access is limited to the operator.

Information is sent to Google/YouTube only as needed for authorized API operations or as required by law.

Retention and deletion

Authorization information is retained only as long as needed for the integration. The operator may revoke access via Google Account connections and delete locally stored tokens. For requests or questions, contact the address below.
